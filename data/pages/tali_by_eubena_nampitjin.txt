====== Tali by Eubena Nampitjin ======

"Tali" by Eubena Nampitjin depicts the sand dunes, or "tali," of the Great Sandy Desert, a landscape deeply connected to her heritage. The artwork uses flowing lines and rich earthy tones to represent the movement and patterns of the dunes shaped by wind and time. Through this piece, Nampitjin brings to life her memories and ancestral connections to the desert, highlighting the beauty and spiritual importance of her homeland.
