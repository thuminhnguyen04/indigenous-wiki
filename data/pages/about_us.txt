====== Welcome to this Wiki ======

This wiki is part of **Indig[e]nizing ICT curriculum: a starter framework for the community of practice**, a collaborative initiative aimed at making computing and digital technologies education more inclusive, relevant, and reflective of First Nations perspectives and knowledge systems.

This initiative and website were developed under a funded grant to support educators, curriculum designers, and communities in embedding Indigenous ways of knowing, being, and doing into ICT and Digital Technologies education.

It is informed by the work of organisations such as the **Australian Council for Computers in Education (ACCE)**—the national professional body supporting the use of information and communication technologies (ICT) in education across Australia. 

This wiki has been created as a **living resource** to:
  * collect and share knowledge, examples, and practices  
  * support the indigenising of ICT and Digital Technologies curriculum  
  * foster a community of practice around culturally responsive computing education  

We hope this space helps educators and learners explore meaningful ways of integrating Indigenous perspectives into teaching and learning in ICT.


===== About the Project =====

This project was funded by the **Australian Council of Deans of ICT (ACDICT)** through its **Small Grants Scheme 2024**.

It is a collaboration between **Deakin University** and **Swinburne University of Technology**, with the goal of developing practical, community-informed approaches to indigenising ICT curriculum across Australian universities.

The project brings together a multidisciplinary team with expertise spanning ICT education, Indigenous studies, and community engagement.
