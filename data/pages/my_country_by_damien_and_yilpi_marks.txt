====== My Country by Damien and Yilpi Marks ======

{{ :mycountry.jpg?400 |}}

"My Country" by Damien and Yilpi Marks celebrates the beauty and spirit of the Australian landscape. Using vibrant colors and layered patterns, the artwork reflects the artists' deep connection to their homeland, portraying rivers, hills, and sacred sites. It’s a visual story of the land’s energy and cultural significance, honoring the ancestral ties and traditions linked to "Country."
