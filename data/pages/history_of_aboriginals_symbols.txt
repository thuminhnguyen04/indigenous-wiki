====== History of Aboriginals ======


===== Aboriginal Symbols Illustration =====
The "U" shape on the ground symbolizes a person sitting cross-legged.  
Lines linking circles illustrate the paths taken by people as they move from one site to another.

===== Women Symbol =====
The combination of a coolamon bowl and a digging stick forms a symbol resembling "UOI", signifying a woman engaged in hunting or gathering.

===== Gathering Symbol =====
The arrangement of spears and possibly boomerangs can be visually represented as "U ||", indicating a male figure.
