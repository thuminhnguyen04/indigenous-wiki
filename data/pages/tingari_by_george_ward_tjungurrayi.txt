====== Tingari by George Ward Tjungurrayi ======

"Tingari" by George Ward Tjungurrayi represents the journeys and rituals of the Tingari ancestors, a group of ancestral beings important in Aboriginal tradition. This artwork captures their travels across the land, leaving sacred sites and knowledge behind, which are crucial for guiding initiations and teaching younger generations. The abstract patterns and concentric circles in the piece echo the sacred landscape, blending spiritual stories with the natural geography of the desert.
